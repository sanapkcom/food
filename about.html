About EATSPOT 🍽️

We help you find the best, most affordable food spots around you

Our Story

EATSPOT was born out of a simple problem — it's hard to find good, affordable food spots in your area, especially when you're a student or someone on a budget. We wanted to build a community-driven platform where anyone could discover and share hidden food gems.

Whether it's that little tea stall by the road, your favourite bakery, or the best biryani joint in town — EATSPOT puts them all on the map so nobody misses out.
